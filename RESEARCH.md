## Career journey
- Building consumer software since the late 1990s; moved to Nashville in the 1990s; worked "for years" in entertainment ([Modern CTO #778](https://moderncto.io/brendan-wovchko-2/)).
- **Founder, ArtistData**: a service letting musicians enter show and profile data once and publish it everywhere, used by 25,000+ artists. Earlier role listed: Sales Engineering Manager, Interspire ([Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/artistdata)).
- Recognized by *Entrepreneur* magazine as a "social networking expert" in May 2008; past work with brands including Avon, Sony, Warner Music and Universal Music ([Scrum Alliance](https://www.scrumalliance.org/members/149057)).
- Held VP Technology, VP Product and CTO roles overseeing product budgets of $1M to $15M ([Scrum Alliance](https://www.scrumalliance.org/members/149057)).
- **Founder, HUGE I/O**: boutique Agile/Lean consultancy and Lean Kanban University accredited training organization ([hugeio.com](https://www.hugeio.com/), [Planview](https://blog.planview.com/author/brendan-wovchko/)).
- Ramsey became his company's biggest customer; he sold the company and joined Ramsey full time, calling it "the first full time job I've ever had" ([Modern CTO #505](https://moderncto.io/brendan-wovchko/)).
- Described as "a lifelong entrepreneur" and "a community organizer".
